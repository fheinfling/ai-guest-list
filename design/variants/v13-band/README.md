# v13-band, relative to v10-ambient

The everyday glance replaces v10's oversized count of ready seats with an edge-to-edge band connecting the active tool and seat on the left to two right-aligned headroom readings: **5-hour above, weekly below**, each explicitly saying **left**. The two hairlines have fixed lengths: they connect the identity to its readings rather than filling up like progress bars. This is the band's job, not a second chart to interpret. The verdict, generous space, rounded system type, guest-list drawer and open-room artwork remain v10's. The fixture has two active tools, so each gets a band; neither is silently chosen as the user's current tool, and inactive seats remain in the drawer. At 376×600 both bands are visible together without a disclosure.

Only `buildHTML` and new glance CSS change. Every other renderer and helper, including `doorKey()` and `doorMark()`, is inherited verbatim. Both consent screens, spending, settings and both pickers render identically to v10; trouble retains its original verdict, count, next-return time and paid-key action, with only the background artwork changed. The palette remains ball mist `#BFE9DF`, Codex mint `#46C2A8`, tile gold `#F7C668`, Claude coral `#E0795A`, twinkle violet `#7C6CF0` and warm glow `#FFF6DF`; `ui-rounded` remains the first font. No new actions, assets, fonts or remote resources were added.

Each window independently converts the supplied usage into remaining headroom. Missing readings and `usage_unknown` say **unknown**, with a dashed hairline; stale readings say **last-known reading** beside the seat. Seat names and plans are escaped. No subscription model is shown or inferred. Pinned and running keys retain v10's literal model IDs on the spending screen. With ready seats but no active selection, the glance says so instead of promoting a default seat.

When `doorKey()` says shut on the availability screen, the inline SVG reuses `app/icon.svg`'s gold tile, warm glow, cream-leaf gradient (`#F5EEDD` to `#D8C8A8`) and `#CF9B2E` knob, with the leaf widened and laid shut. **At 510px, the closed leaf becomes the room behind the verdict, with its knob giving the large quiet shape an immediately recognisable scale.** It replaces the disco ball there, sits behind all content, ignores pointer input, and is hidden from assistive technology. Normal compositing at 60% opacity in light and 7% in dark preserves conservative text-contrast lower bounds of **7.45:1 light / 4.95:1 dark**, calculated against the darkest light-art colour and a pure-white dark-art bound over the worst field stops. Consent and spending retain their inherited artwork and colour fields.
